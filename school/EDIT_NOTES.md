# School edition: what changed from the manuscript, and why

Checked against Butcher & Lang (Gutenberg #1728, Arc library). Quotes are theirs. All three scenes were rewritten almost line by line;
the original scenes stay untouched in `ATHENA_manuscript.md`. Lines kept from the machine draft are listed under each scene.

Removed everywhere: Muse welcomes ("Ladies and Gentlemen", "esteemed patrons", "diners"), toasts, "Turn N" and emoji labels, song names,
hooks tied to songs, and the fragments that echoed real lyrics ("What is this that unfolds... (Oh, no)", the crimson cloak, "New Rules:", "pour one out for my bad habits", "My type's a predator").

## Book IX: Nobody (was "Bad Habits")
| Manuscript | Homer | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Wine "from grapes that grow on Crete's own shore", mixed with honey | "dark wine and sweet which Maron... the priest of Apollo" gave him at Ismarus; one cup to "twenty measures of water" | Maron, Apollo's priest, one cup in twenty |
| Polyphemus tells "his secret" | No secret | Cut |
| Odysseus disowns his crew ("ain't no friends of mine") | He pleads for them all | Cut |
| "Poseidon's son, blind and wild" before the blinding | Not yet blind | Cut |
| Polyphemus knows who he is right after the blinding ("I know who you really are") | Learns the name only when it is shouted from the ship | Moved to the ship |
| A "transformation", power "seeping" out, Cyclops "powerless" | None; he hurls two rocks and prays | Cut |
| Missing | Crew beg him to take the cheese; the sword he doesn't use; six men in all; the Cyclopes' "Nobody" joke; the rams; "Dear ram"; the crew begging on the ship; the exact curse | All restored |

Kept from the draft: "RUMBLE, ROAR", "CRUNCH", "Men between teeth, no mercy in hunger", "This cave mine".

## Book V: Calypso's Offer (was "Calypso's Island", song "My Type")
| Manuscript | Homer | Now |
|---|---|---|
| "Ten years on this island" | "abode for seven years" (Book VII) | Seven |
| Calypso a "sea-witch" | "the nymph", "that fair goddess" | Goddess |
| Odysseus in the grotto, hears Hermes | He "sat weeping on the shore"; Hermes never meets him | On the shore |
| Hermes "vanished", then returns | One visit | One visit |
| "where was Zeus when I bore him this one?" (nonsense), "He sacks my cities" | She saved him from the keel after Zeus's bolt; gods are "jealous exceeding"; Orion and Iasion | Her real argument |
| Sirens-style "sisterhood" chorus | Not in Homer | Cut |
| "ship of vengeance", "I'll reclaim my throne" | Asks her oath, refuses immortality, knows Penelope is "meaner to look upon" and wants home anyway | His real answer |
| Missing | The oath by the Styx; supper; the offer; 20 trees, 4 days, the Bear on his left, 17 days, Poseidon | All restored |

Kept from the draft: "It's not a boast, just stating the news", "that's all there is to say".

**A creative call for Ross:** the original brief reframed the island ("not a prison, a morally murky partnership", most of ten years here, sacking cities for her). That contradicts Homer, so the school edition follows Homer. Calypso keeps her agency and her point: "You call this a prison. I call it a shore" is now her argument, and prompt 2 asks students to judge it.

## Book XXIII: The Olive Tree (was "New Rules")
| Manuscript | Homer | Now |
|---|---|---|
| "The bed that moves with us"; the nurse leads the beggar to the bed | The bed can't move. She orders it moved, as a trap | The real test |
| "Anchored to the earth by Calypso's tears" | Built round a rooted olive | Rooted olive |
| Odysseus calm, recognition comes because she sees the bed | He is furious, and his anger proves it; "her knees were loosened" | His anger |
| She "knew in that moment" then raps a long lament, feels "betrayed" | "Be not angry with me"; she feared being fooled by men's words | Her real speech |
| Telemachus missing | Rebukes her: "harder than stone" | Restored |
| Missing | Nurse's scar proof, the long silence, the bath, the swimmer simile, Athena holding back the dawn | All restored |

Kept from the draft: "That bed's my own design, an olive tree its spine"; "she decides when this reunion happens, not him".

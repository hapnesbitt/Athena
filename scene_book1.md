# ATHENA — Book I: Deal With It

*Immersive dinner theater.*

---
### MUSE 🎩

Welcome, dear diners of Ithaca, tonight's spectacle unfolds within these very walls. You've feasted for twenty years on a banquet of decadence, while the heart of this house lay in ruins. Tonight, the boy who's been told to "deal with it" stands up. The suitor, the feaster... becomes the feasted. Can you feel the tension rising? This is not a story about passivity—this is a tale of compressed rage finding its release. Let the games begin. The palace of Ithaca awaits.

---

### Turn 1 — ANTINOUS 🍷

[STAGING: Antinous sprawls at the head table, raising his cup to the room, ignoring Telemachus entirely]

ANTINOUS:
Y'all know me, Antinous, King of Ithaca's heart.
Twenty years gone, Odysseus a ghost, a work of art.
I've run this castle like a boss, no need to debate,
A throne left empty, ain't that plain as day?

[STAGING: Antinous points at Telemachus, who stands nervously by the door]

ANTINOUS:
Little boy playing grown man's game. What do you think you bring to the table?
Fear not, Telemachus, I'll make sure you won't be able
To spoil my feast with your childish whine and plea.
Your father's dead, accept it, it's time for you to flee.

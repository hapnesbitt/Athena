# ATHENA — Book I: Deal With It

*Immersive dinner theater.*

---
### MUSE 🎩

Ladies and Gentlemen, welcome to Book I of ATHENA - an immersive dinner-theater retelling of the Odyssey. Tonight, we step into the decadent palace of Ithaca, where the suitors have feasted for twenty years, devouring your hard-won bounty like vultures circling a carcass. The boy of the house, Telemachus, has been a passive pawn in this game, but that's about to change.

Now, some of you have dined and danced with these suitors, clapped them on the back, even smiled at their lewd jokes. You've watched as Penelope, the queen, endured their relentless advances, her spirit unbroken yet. But tonight, the boy is finding his first anger, and he won't be dealt with lightly.

So, lean in, dear diners. This house has secrets, and we're about to uncover one. You've bought into this wager, and now it's time to see who gets to go home - and what they'll have to pay for it.

Remember, these are your courtiers, your Ithacans. They've played their parts, but tonight, the boy stands up. And so will you, when the curtain rises. Let's see if you can deal with it.

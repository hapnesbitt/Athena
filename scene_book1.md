# ATHENA — Book I: Deal With It

*Immersive dinner theater.*

---
### MUSE 🎩

Welcome, dear diners of Ithaca, tonight's spectacle unfolds within these very walls. You've feasted for twenty years on a banquet of decadence, while the heart of this house lay in ruins. Tonight, the boy who's been told to "deal with it" stands up. The suitor, the feaster... becomes the feasted. Can you feel the tension rising? This is not a story about passivity—this is a tale of compressed rage finding its release. Let the games begin. The palace of Ithaca awaits.

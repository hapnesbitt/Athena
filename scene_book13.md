# ATHENA — Book XIII: WOW

*Immersive dinner theater.*

---
### MUSE 🎩

Ladies and gentlemen, we reach the climactic chapter of our epic saga, Book XIII! The Phaeacians have delivered our hero, Odysseus, home after two decades. But don't be fooled by his royal air as he steps ashore—tonight, Athena will transform him into a humble beggar for the long game ahead. Let the swagger of a king in disguise unfold before you, as post-Malone's 'WOW' sets the stage for our king's return! Let's dive into the witty banter between two cunning players—the goddess and her champion. So, gather round, my friends—welcome to Odysseus's homecoming, where the stakes have never been higher!

---

### Turn 1 — ODYSSEUS 🗡️

[STAGING: Odysseus wakes alone on the shore; the mist lifts; he touches the ground of Ithaca]

"Home, at last... but it don't feel right.
My heart beats faster than a Phaeacian knight.
I'm home, yeah, I can taste the salt air,
But the echoes of war still fill the air.

My bones know this soil, deep in my soul,
This is where I belong, though it takes its toll.
Penelope, Telemachus, what waits for me?
A kingdom torn by time and treachery.

I'll play this game, wear this beggar's skin,
But don't you worry 'bout me, I'll rise again.
The gods may turn their back, but I can't forget,
Home is here, but this war ain't over yet."

### Turn 2 — ATHENA 🦉

[STAGING: Athena emerges from the sea, a shimmering vision]

ATHENA:
Yo, my king, Odysseus, crowned in wisdom and grit.
I've come for you, for the battle that we're about to sit.
You'll play this game disguised, a beggar's skin you'll wear,
Infiltrate the court, gather allies near.

[STAGING: Athena passes her hand over Odysseus; the king becomes a ragged old beggar]

This long game begins, brother in arms and divine,
We'll strike when the moment is ripe, no time to unwind.
I've got your back, you know this, so trust in my might,
Together we'll end this war, banishing darkness from sight.

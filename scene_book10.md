# ATHENA — Book X: Circe's Hall

*Immersive dinner theater.*

---
### MUSE 🎩

[STAGE: dim lights, low rumble of bass]

Ladies and gentlemen, esteemed guests, welcome to Circe's Hall! The witching hour approaches, and we're about to dive deep into the enchanting, bewitching world of our sorceress host. Now, keep your wits about you, because tonight, some of you might just find yourselves transformed. Not by a spell, mind you, but by the magic of our immersive theater!

[STAGING: dancers begin moving among the tables, choosing their men]

So sit back, relax, and enjoy the show as we venture into Circe's dance floor, where the music is seductive, the stakes are high, and the night is far from ordinary. Let the games begin!

---

### Turn 1 — HERMES 🪽

[STAGING: Hermes swoops down from the shadows, approaching Odysseus with a knowing nod]

ATHENA:
Listen close, city-sacker, this I bring for you—
Moly, an herb to break Circe's wicked brew.
Her potion won't touch your blood tonight,
This gift from Zeus'll see you through the fight.
[STAGING: Hermes slips Odysseus the moly and vanishes]

### Turn 2 — CIRCE 🪄

(In a low, commanding purr)

Yass, now gather 'round,
My realm is yours, make no doubt.
Step onto my floor, bow down,
And let your souls be bound.

[STAGING: dancers move among the tables and bring three men up from the audience as Odysseus's crew]

I am Circe, sorceress supreme,
Don't test me, boys, don't dare to dream.
Each man I choose, each form I bend,
A dance with fate that never ends.

Bodies at my feet, they heed the call,
This is power, this is all.
Don't stop, don't quit, I reign above,
Watch Circe's magic take its hold.

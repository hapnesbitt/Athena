# ATHENA — Book V: Calypso's Island

*Immersive dinner theater.*

---
### MUSE 🎩

Ladies and gentlemen, welcome! You've entered Calypso's domain, her island a testament to the power of desire and seduction. The gods may have their secrets, but tonight we peel back the veil. Tonight, we witness a dance as old as time itself - predator meets predator in a game of cat and mouse.

Saweetie's got the right idea when she sings "My Type." Odysseus, the master sacker of cities from the sea, has found his match in Calypso, the sea-witch who lures him with her charms. But as the night unfolds, the stakes grow higher, and Odysseus must choose between his insatiable appetite and the home he's been longing for.

So grab your wine, settle in, and prepare to witness the turning point in our immersive retelling of Homer's epic saga, as Athena works her magic, and the journey home finally begins. But remember, this island is not a prison - it's a place where partnerships are forged, and sacrifices are made. Let the games begin!

---

### Turn 1 — HERMES 🪽

With a gust of wind that stirs the night, I descend,
Hermes, Zeus's swift emissary, on bended knee I bend,
"Odysseus must leave, Zeus commands the journey start anew,
Calypso, release your captive, his home awaits him true."

I take flight with words that sting, a messenger's winged mission done,
The dance of gods continues, under Calypso's island sun.

### Turn 2 — CALYPSO 🌊

Calypso, the sea-witch sovereign, scoffs with haughty grace,
A twisted smile graces her lips as she gazes at Hermes' face,
She speaks with a hiss that could freeze the Aegean breeze,
"Dismissed, impudent messenger, by Zeus' decree? I disagree!"

Her voice rings out like the echo of thunderous waves,
As Calypso's eyes gleam like the emerald jewels she saves,
"Odysseus, my type, is no mere plaything for gods to toy,
I will not release him, not yet, or else suffer the boy."

With a sinister laugh that echoes through Calypso's realm,
She vows to keep her prize, and make those gods pay their dues,
In this game of cat-and-mouse, it seems Calypso will prove true,
A predator as cunning as any who have come before.

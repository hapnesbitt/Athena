But fear not, our hero is about to be released, torn between his insatiable appetite and the longing call of home. And remember, this isn't just a prison - it's a dance floor where gods play their cards close to their chests. So sit back, hold on tight, and let the music guide you through this turning point in our Odyssean journey.

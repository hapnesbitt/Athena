Yo, wealthy dynasty! Welcome to BOOK V: CALYPSO'S ISLAND! The night our man Odysseus finally breaks free from his sea-witch lover's clutches. Tonight's number is sizzling hot - Calypso's "My Type," a dance off that'll leave you breathless, just like when she lured our hero to her island with her hypnotic charm. But remember, beneath the sultry melodies, this ain't no love story - it's a tale of power and control, of an ancient predator meeting his match.

Now, we all know that Odysseus has been sacking cities from the sea, hoping to go home rich. And Calypso... well, she's got a point, don'tcha think? So strap in, diners - the stakes are high, and the dance floor is about to light up! Let's bring on the gods!

### Turn 1 — HERMES 🪽

Hermes, swift-footed messenger of Zeus, descends from the heavens in a whirlwind of feathers and light. Calypso's island glimmers before him like a diamond in the azure sea. He lands with a soft thud, his wings folded close to his back as he strides towards the sea-witch's lair.

"Yo, Calypso," he calls out, "Zeus has sent me with an order. Odysseus must be released, the journey home begins now." A hint of a smile plays on his lips as he awaits her response, the air between them electric with tension.

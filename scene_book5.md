### MUSE 🎩

Ladies and Gentlemen, welcome to Book V of ATHENA, where we delve into Calypso's Island, a realm where the predator meets her match. Tonight, we bear witness to a partnership not a prison, a dance between Odysseus and Calypso, each bound by their own desires.

Odysseus, the man who sacked cities from the sea, has found himself ensnared in Calypso's web, longing for home while staying rich. But tonight, the game changes. Hermes brings Zeus's decree, setting our hero free.

As the stakes rise, so does the tension. Will Odysseus resist Calypso's allure or succumb to the call of home? Prepare yourselves for a performance that will leave you breathless, as we dive into the heart of this timeless tale. Let's raise our glasses and toast to the journey that awaits us! Now, please, allow me to introduce Calypso, who sings her anthem, "My Type." Get ready to feel the rhythm beneath the ancient words!

### Turn 1 — HERMES 🪽

Zeus's decree in hand, I touch the earth, a gust of wind and I'm here,
The God of messengers, Hermes, no time for cheer.
A somber note, but it's time to state the truth,
Odysseus, the city-sacker's reign must end, his journey starts anew.
I speak for Zeus, not my own words, so take heed,
The Goddess Calypso, let Odysseus be freed.
His journey home begins, let's see if he'll obey,
But this ain't the last you'll hear of me or what I have to say.

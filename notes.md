## ===== CASTING PRINCIPLE (structural spine) =====
# Athena is MOSTLY WOMEN SINGING — Odysseus does the BRIDGES between scenes.
# WHY: (1) hip-hop dance classes are mostly women — this is built for them to perform.
#      (2) It's also the TRUE shape of the Odyssey — a man passing through a series of
#          powerful women (Athena, Calypso, Circe, Sirens, Nausicaa, Penelope). The women
#          hold the power in each episode; Odysseus is the throughline moving between them.
# ROLE STRUCTURE:
#   - Each scene's powerful FEMALE figure = the lead singer / gets the song.
#   - ODYSSEUS = the bridges/transitions — connective verses carrying us scene to scene.
#     He's acted UPON by goddess after goddess; he's the tissue, not the star of any number.

## ===== ARTISTIC THESIS (the WHY behind it all) =====
# "Athena hands the Odyssey to its women."
# Most adaptations center Odysseus — his cunning, his trials, the camera follows the man.
# Athena INVERTS this: the women are the SUBJECTS. Each (Athena, Calypso, Circe, Sirens,
# Nausicaa, Penelope) is the hero of her own scene, with her own number and power.
# Odysseus is demoted to connective tissue — the bridge between the women who each, in turn,
# own the stage. This isn't revisionism: in Homer the women genuinely hold the power in each
# episode (a goddess's help, a witch's mercy, a queen's hospitality). Centering them is just
# reading what's already there. The dance-class reality (mostly women) IS the artistic truth.

## ===== THE MUSE IS THE DEEP THESIS (oral tradition restored) =====
# THE INSIGHT (Ross): the Muse role was the KEY to performing the Odyssey in oral tradition
# 3,000-2,500 years ago. This is not a flourish — it's the spine of the whole project.
#
# WHY: The Odyssey opens by INVOKING the Muse ("Sing to me of the man, Muse...") because the
# oral bard (aoidos) WAS the Muse's voice. He didn't recite a fixed text — he RE-COMPOSED the
# poem live each performance using formulae and epithets ("rosy-fingered dawn," "wine-dark sea")
# — repeated building blocks that let one performer GENERATE the next lines in real time and
# hold an audience through a story too long to memorize verbatim. The Muse was the GENERATIVE
# ENGINE that made the performance possible: filling, bridging, carrying the narrative.
#
# THE REALIZATION: the clb engine IS this mechanism, rebuilt and pointed back at its own source.
#   - character-agents = aoidoi (bards)
#   - voice bible = the formulaic tradition
#   - the Muse/MC generating connective patter live = the literal job of the ancient bard
# Athena doesn't ADAPT the Odyssey to AI — it uses AI to RETURN the Odyssey to the performance
# mode it was BORN in: a generative oral performance held together by an invoked generative voice.
# The women embody the episodes (dance); the Muse generates the connective tissue (patter).
# It breathes the way it did around a fire 3,000 years ago, before it was frozen into fixed text.
#
## ===== PRACTICAL: the MC patter is LOAD-BEARING (Cupcake production reality) =====
# Cupcake shows: dancers get ZERO breaks — instant costume changes, nearly everyone in every
# skit, few spare dancers (only a few 2-dancer skits). So the MC's patter BUYS TIME — it holds
# the room with entertaining/naughty/silly pre-scripted material WHILE dancers change (~60-90s).
# => The Muse's bridges must be SIZED for this: substantial, charming, time-filling MC segments
#    that cover a costume change, not 20-second transitions. The engine already produces the right
#    KIND of thing (verse w/ personality) — tune it to know the JOB: cover the change, hold the
#    room, be funny. This is also exactly the ancient bard's function: fill and hold between the
#    parts that can't pause.
